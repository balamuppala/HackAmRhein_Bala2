Basel-Stadt • Policy Adopted 2021

Stadtklimakonzept

Basel-Stadt's comprehensive plan for a cooler, more breathable city. Pushes aggressive tree canopy growth, artificial shade, unsealing ground, and protecting vital cold-air ventilation corridors (*Kaltluftschneisen*).

Priority Focus Zones:
Inner City St. Johann Gundeldingen Matthäus Klybeck

Basel-Stadt • Sponge Design Active Pilots

Schwammstadt Basel

The core urban sponge principle: keep rain local so it soaks into deep soil, evaporates to cool ambient air, or is stored for reuse during drought. VoltaNord was named the first area comprehensively designed this way, following early unsealing at Erlenmatt «Im Triangel».

Key Sites:

VoltaNord (Infiltration trenches + Biochar) & Erlenmatt «Im Triangel»

Basel-Stadt • Combined Works Infrastructure

Grüne Zukunft

Unsealing asphalt and tree planting bundled directly with IWB utility extensions for district heating. Coordinates street Digging with street Greenery.

Therwilerstrasse & Paulusgasse: Converting asphalt to sponge beds.
Wettsteinallee: Gaining 10 new shade trees and permeable grass-joint parking slots.

Basel-Stadt • Building Law Mandatory

Grüne Dächer für Basel

Municipal ordinance requiring all unused flat roofs on new builds and major renovations to be planted with greenery. Requires deeper substrate layers to store maximum rainwater and throttled drainage outlets to prevent storm surge flooding.

Retains peak storm runoff while providing biodiversity habitats across rooftop corridors.
Green Roof Mandate Baugesetz Regulations
Basel-Stadt • Permit Policy Free Permit

Fassadenbegrünung auf Allmend

Allows private property owners to plant climbing greenery along their building facades even when the planter base rests on public pavement (*Allmend*).

Process streamlined via simplified application; the permit is entirely free of administrative charge.

Riehen • Municipal Strategy Adopted Feb 2024

Klimakonzept Riehen

Formally adopted in February 2024. Tackles extreme summer heat and torrential downpours using integrated sponge city concepts—storing surface rainwater in natural basins to nourish municipal greenery during heatwaves.

Dual Protection: Microclimate evaporation cooling + rainwater retention basins.
Gemeinde Riehen riehen.ch
Allschwil (BL) • Pilot Aug 2026 Expansion

Mein Baum, dein Baum

Community pilot planting large shade trees in private front gardens. Modelled directly after Basel's earlier scheme, which successfully planted ~200 private shade trees to combat neighborhood heat islands.

Impact: 200+ trees added to urban canopy across private property borders.

Canton Basel-Landschaft's umbrella adaptation program featuring targeted heat action plans, technical guidance for suburban municipalities, and systematic spot checks for rainwater retention capacity.

Cross-Border (DE) • Municipal Mandate Passed March 2024

Freiburg Rainwater Concept

Neighboring Freiburg's *Klimaanpassungskonzept Regenwasser*. Differentiates ordinary precipitation (must be retained 100% on-site) from extreme cloudbursts. Required by city council in all land-use planning since March 2024.

Statutory land-use mandate separating normal infiltration from emergency overland flow.
